# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone who contributes to this project, regardless of their background, experience, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and professional.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Helping other contributors.
- Focusing on what is best for the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Insulting or offensive comments.
- Personal attacks.
- Trolling or deliberately disrupting discussions.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take appropriate corrective action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, and other communication channels.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

All complaints will be reviewed and handled fairly and appropriately.
